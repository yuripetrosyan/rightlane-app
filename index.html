Support

Questions, or something in a lesson that looks wrong? Email ypetrosyan17@gmail.com. Please include the question and, if you can, a screenshot.

I paid, but Pro is locked

Open Settings in Rightlane and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Rightlane. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this the official CBR app?

No. Rightlane is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with the CBR, the RDW or the Dutch government.
