Privacy policy

Rightlane for iPhone and iPad. Last changed on 24 November 2026.

Rightlane has no accounts. Your lessons, answers and results stay on your device and are never sent to us.

What leaves your device

Purchases. Rightlane uses RevenueCat to unlock and restore Rightlane Pro. When the app opens and when you view, buy or restore Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt from Apple, the product you bought, and technical data such as your IP address and device model. This is needed to give you what you paid for. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Usage analytics, only if you turn it on. Usage analytics is off when you install Rightlane. If you turn on “Share usage analytics” in Settings, Google Firebase Analytics receives usage events, such as that the Pro screen was shown or a purchase was started, with an app instance ID, your device model, iOS version, language, and the country or region Google estimates from your IP address. The events never contain your answers, results, name or email. You can turn it off again at any time.

RevenueCat and Google may process this data in the United States under the safeguards in their own terms: RevenueCat, Google. Rightlane shows no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Progress, answers, exam results, your exam date and the signs you have found. Home Screen widgets and the Lock Screen show part of this, on your device only. Device backups you make, such as iCloud Backup, may include it.
  • Sign Lens: the camera picture, or a photo you choose, is read on your device. It is not stored or sent.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device.
  • A note in the device’s Keychain of which free tries you have used. It remains when you reset progress or delete the app.
  • The tilt of your device, used to move the view of the road slightly. It is not stored or sent.
  • Lesson and sign titles, so that search on your device can find them.

Your choices and rights

  • “Reset progress” in Settings deletes your lessons, answers, review schedule and exam results. Deleting the app deletes them too. Neither removes the Keychain note or copies in backups you made.
  • Turn usage analytics on or off in Settings.
  • You may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data held about you, such as the purchase data RevenueCat keeps under your anonymous ID. Email us. You may also complain to your data protection authority, in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Young learners

Rightlane is for people learning to drive. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before turning on usage analytics.
